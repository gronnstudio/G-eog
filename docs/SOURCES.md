# Sources Registry

A central registry of external sources used to research and expand the
Equilibrium knowledge hub. These are **discovery starting points for
contributors** — places to find species information, materials, practitioners
and field data. Content in the hub is always rephrased original writing that
cites primary literature; the sites below are research entry points, **not
citation targets**.

## How to add a source

Open a pull request adding a row to the relevant table (or a new group) with
the URL, language, and one line on why it is useful to Equilibrium.

## Plant & species information (NL)

|---|---|---|---|
| Plantenzoeker | [www.plantenzoeker.com](https://www.plantenzoeker.com) | NL | Search engine for nurseries, garden centres and plant webshops with their main plant offering — useful for checking availability and naming of species covered in articles. |
